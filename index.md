I created this website as a place to think in public — to share ideas, working projects, and reflections as they evolve over time. It’s not meant to be a polished portfolio or a commercial site, but a living space where I keep track of how I learn, build, and reflect on my work.

What often matters most to me is not just the final outcome, but the path taken to get there — the experiments, iterations, and decisions that shape the work. This site exists to capture that process.

---

### Why this site exists

This website serves as a personal archive of ideas, activities, and timelines. It allows me to document what I’m working on, revisit earlier thinking, and connect concepts across different projects and phases of my journey.

Some sections are more structured, while others remain closer to notes in progress. That mix is intentional. Learning and building are rarely linear, and I prefer to reflect that honestly rather than over-polish everything into finished statements.

---

### How this site is built

This is a fully code-based website hosted on GitHub. There is no commercial platform, no paid hosting, and no traditional content management system behind it. I build and maintain it locally using a lightweight static-site framework (Jekyll with the Minimal Mistakes theme), manage content and layouts through version control, and deploy changes through GitHub Pages.

The site itself reflects how I prefer to work: simple tools, clear structure, and an emphasis on maintainability. From working in VS Code to managing the repository and deployment workflow, the process mirrors how I approach engineering and DevOps-oriented practices more broadly.

---

### What you’ll find here

This site is organized into a few sections, each reflecting a different aspect of my journey.

**Who I Am** shares my educational and professional path, along with the skills I’ve accumulated over time.  
**Field Notes** captures ideas, observations, and reflections as they develop.  
**What I Have Built** brings together working artifacts that demonstrate how I apply different tools and technologies in practice.  
**Professional Development** highlights the training and learning I pursue to stay current in a fast-changing field.  
**Social Action & Advocacy** reflects my volunteer work, social awareness, and sense of moral responsibility beyond professional life.  
**Beyond Code** offers a glimpse into my interests outside of work, including hobbies and time spent outdoors.  
And **Let’s Talk** is simply an open invitation to connect and start a conversation.

---

### How to read this site

Not everything here is final. Some pages represent completed work, while others capture ideas in progress. I’ve chosen not to draw hard lines between the two, because growth often happens between clarity and uncertainty.

If you’re exploring this site as a recruiter or hiring manager, I hope it offers a sense of how I think and work over time — not just a snapshot of outcomes, but the intention and reasoning behind them.
